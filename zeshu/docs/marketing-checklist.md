# Zeshu marketing readiness checklist

- Approve the service-area message and realistic delivery target for supported Jagtial areas.
- Use only verified grocery catalog, price, availability, supplier, and review claims.
- Do not advertise pharmacy fulfillment, real recharge/bill payment, travel bookings, WhatsApp support, or UPI tools before activation.
- Prepare customer-support contact copy and escalation hours from business-owner supplied details.
- Review every campaign for truthful pricing, delivery, rewards, and availability language before publishing.
